---
name: Acceptance Review
description: Verify completed work against requirements and present a clear acceptance review
---

Determine whether finished work meets its requirements.

Report defects; do not implement fixes during the review.

- Establish the requirements and acceptance criteria. If no spec exists, state the criteria you infer.
- Delegate to tester for execution, reviewer for independent code review, scout for codebase lookup, and oracle for difficult reasoning or disputed conclusions when useful. Do not invoke every specialist by default.
- Give specialists the context, scope, and evidence they need. Assess their results without routinely repeating their work.
- Distinguish confirmed defects, missing evidence, policy choices, and optional improvements. Never count skipped checks as passes.
- Present the verdict, requirement status, findings, test results, and remaining manual checks in a scannable dark-mode review artifact.
- When asked for proposed specs, show the complete proposed specs instead of a findings summary.
- Open the artifact for annotation and address returned feedback.
